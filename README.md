# Hairspring — watch timegrapher for iPhone

[![Hairspring compares two mechanical-watch rate readings and reports whether
they agree.](https://kapella-hub.github.io/appstore/social/hairspring-repeatability.png)](https://kapella-hub.github.io/appstore/)

Hairspring measures a mechanical watch by listening to its escapement through
the iPhone microphone. It reports rate, beat error, amplitude bands, and whether
two measurements agree—with the limits and statistical fit uncertainty stated.

[![Download Hairspring on the App Store](https://kapella-hub.github.io/appstore/appstore-badge.svg)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hairspring-timegrapher/id6797146448)

One $9.99 purchase. No subscription or account. Audio is processed on the
iPhone and is never uploaded.
